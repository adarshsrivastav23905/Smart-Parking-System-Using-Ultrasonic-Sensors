FINAL RESULT

The Smart Parking System successfully detects the occupancy
status of four parking slots using HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensors.

The system successfully:
- Measures parking-slot distance
- Detects FREE/OCCUPIED status
- Counts available slots
- Displays availability on LCD
- Controls green/red indicators
- Activates the buzzer when parking is full

The complete prototype was successfully tested using Wokwi
virtual simulation.
